## Prompt

Create an English presentation titled **“Rice AI Platform: Web Application Development”** with **exactly 6 slides**. The presentation must take **less than 7 minutes**. Use simple English, short sentences, and common words that are easy to read and speak.

The presenter is the **web application developer**. Do not present the presenter as the creator of the AI model or the Dashboard data.

### Team Roles

- **Presenter’s work:** Web application design and development, UX/UI, mobile responsive design, user pages, admin system, login and access control, API and database integration, and data display.
- **Teammate’s work:** The rice disease AI model and the datasets and analysis used in the rice yield Dashboard.
- The presenter connected the teammate’s model and data to the website and made them easy for users to access.
- Never say that the presenter trained the AI model, created the prediction algorithm, or collected the Dashboard datasets.

### Audience and Style

- Audience: teachers, project judges, and people interested in the project.
- Tone: clear, confident, natural, and not too technical.
- Use the current Rice AI Platform theme and font.
- Use dark green, cream, mint green, and light gold from the existing website.
- Use real screenshots of the website as the main visuals.
- Keep text short. Use large titles and clear visual hierarchy.
- Put speaking time only in the speaker notes, not on the slides.

## Speaker Notes Rules

- Total speaking time should be about **5 minutes 30 seconds to 6 minutes 15 seconds**.
- The full presentation must not exceed 7 minutes.
- Use short sentences and easy English.
- Avoid difficult technical terms.
- Do not read every bullet word for word.
- Add a natural transition between slides.
- Do not say that the presenter created or trained the AI model.
- Do not say that the presenter created or collected the Dashboard data.

## Credit and Accuracy Rules

- When discussing disease analysis, say: **“The website connects to the AI model developed by my teammate.”**
- When discussing the Dashboard, say: **“The website displays data and analysis provided by my teammate.”**
- Use **developed** for the presenter’s web application work.
- Use **connected** or **displayed** for the teammate’s AI model and data.
- Do not invent model accuracy, user numbers, business results, or other statistics.
- If no verified number is available, use a clear qualitative statement instead.

## Final Output Rules

- Exactly 6 slides
- English language
- Simple and easy-to-read wording
- Speaker notes on every slide
- Use real Rice AI Platform screenshots when available
- Do not add separate Agenda, Q&A, or Thank You slides
- Check grammar and spelling before export
